# DNS mean profiles: El Khoury et al. (2013)

`550_Re_1.dat` and `1000_Re_1.dat` are unmodified copies from the Zenodo
record [10.5281/zenodo.7352880](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7352880),
"Turbulent pipe flow up to Re_tau = 1000". They were downloaded on
2026-11-05 and are licensed [CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Each file's own header gives the case (Re_b, Re_τ, u_τ/U_b), the
normalisation and the column order: (1−r), (1−r)⁺, U_z⁺, U_z/U_b, the rms
velocities, ⟨u_z u_r⟩⁺ and dU_z/dr⁺.

Cite: G. K. El Khoury, P. Schlatter, A. Noorani, P. F. Fischer,
G. Brethouwer and A. V. Johansson, "Direct numerical simulation of
turbulent pipe flow at moderately high Reynolds numbers", *Flow, Turbulence
and Combustion* 91 (2013) 475–495 (`el_khoury_and_schlatter_etal_2013`
in `docs/references.bib`).
