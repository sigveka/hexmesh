# hexmesh gallery

What hexmesh's meshes look like and how good their cells are, on three pages.  Every example is a runnable module; the pages are generated by `docs/gallery/build.py` (prose from the examples' docstrings, numbers and images from meshing them).

- **[Features](gallery/FEATURES.md):** the building blocks, by family (7 families, 25 examples): bends, reducers, junctions, sections, layers, grading, and how networks are built.
- **[Applications](gallery/APPLICATIONS.md):** 6 networks closer to a real model, and which fittings they use.
- **[Validation](gallery/VALIDATION.md):** 13 cases solved and compared with a reference, judged by grid convergence.

## Reading the numbers

- **Cells:** hexahedra in the written mesh.  hexmesh predicts the count before GMSH runs (`Assembly.cell_count()`); a ✗ marks a mismatch.
- **SICN:** GMSH's signed inverse condition number, per cell: 1 for a cube, 0 or below for an inverted cell.  Examples must have none at or below 0.
- **SJ:** the minimum scaled Jacobian over a hex's corners (1 for a cube), with the fitting holding the worst cell.  Junctions (tee, wye) are where it is lowest.
- **Aspect:** the largest ratio of a cell's longest to shortest edge.  Wall grading and long axial cells raise it deliberately.
- **Build:** wall time of `main()` (build, mesh, write, quality check) on the machine in the page's footer.  Compare within one run only.
- **Images:** the network from outside (walls grey, open ends coloured by material), and the inlet cross-section seen from upstream.
